THE LOSS OF US MILITARY DOMINANCE IN THE MIDDLE EAST

Cause → Effect: The critical exhaustion of US precision weapons and air defense systems during a five-month conflict with Iran → Washington's forced covert capitulation and the de facto transfer of control over the region's strategic trade routes to regional players.

The superficial media narrative paints a picture of diplomatic success and an imminent peace treaty initiated by the Trump administration. In reality, the United States collided with an insurmountable mathematical barrier in maintaining its military hegemony. The five-month campaign against Iran led to a catastrophic depletion of American arsenals: approximately 80% of THAAD interceptor stockpiles and 50% of Patriot systems were expended, along with virtually the entire available arsenal of long-range missiles, including ATACMS and Precision Strike Missiles.

This signifies more than a temporary tactical deficit; it represents the complete collapse of the American doctrine of remote deterrence. The Iranian leadership, perfectly aware of this vulnerability and Washington's lack of political will to launch a full-scale ground operation, successfully seized the strategic initiative.

  • Depletion of Air Defense Systems (THAAD): ~80% expended. Analytical Interpretation: Loss of capability to repel massive missile strikes in the Middle East.
  • Depletion of Air Defense Systems (Patriot): ~50% expended. Analytical Interpretation: Decreased global operational readiness, leaving allies in Europe and Asia vulnerable.
  • Strike Missiles (ATACMS, PSM): Practically exhausted. Analytical Interpretation: Inability to strike hardened targets deep inside Iranian territory without deploying aircraft.
  • Status of the Strait of Hormuz: Negotiations via Oman. Analytical Interpretation: Legitimization of Iran's right to control a key artery of the global energy market.

THE MONOPOLIZATION OF PHYSICAL AI INFRASTRUCTURE

Cause → Effect: The collapse of the speculative segment of AI startups amid massive capital expenditures by Big Tech on hardware and energy → The redistribution of real power from software developers to providers of physical infrastructure, silicon chips, and energy.

A fundamental shift is underway in the technology sector. The true artificial intelligence boom lies not in generative language models, but in the monopolization of global computing and energy capacities. Amazon, which topped the Fortune Global 500 with $716.9 billion in revenue and $77.7 billion in profit, is directing an unprecedented $131 billion in 2025 and $200 billion in 2026 exclusively into AWS infrastructure and the development of its own chips (Graviton, Trainium).

Simultaneously, we observe a convergence of the IT sector and traditional heavy industry. Oilfield services giant SLB is transforming into a key provider of modular data centers and digital energy management systems for AI hyperscalers, capitalizing on the fact that data centers require colossal volumes of energy. These corporations, controlling the base layer (hardware and energy), are becoming the new sovereigns, invulnerable to political fluctuations.

  • Amazon (US): 2025 Revenue: $716.9B. Key Strategic Vector: $200B CapEx (2026); independence in AI chip production (Trainium); orbital internet (Amazon Leo).
  • Apple (US): 2025 Revenue: $416.2B. Key Strategic Vector: OS-level AI integration and distribution control via the App Store.
  • SLB (US/Curaçao): 2025 Revenue: $35.7B. Key Strategic Vector: Modular data center construction; autonomous drilling; hyperscaler power grid management.

COMMERCIALIZATION AND INSTITUTIONAL CAPTURE OF THE US STATE

Cause → Effect: The appointment of absolute loyalists to key posts (Fed, DOJ) and the introduction of mechanisms to directly monetize access to the president → The final dismantling of US institutional independence and the transition to a model of open oligarchic corruption.

The state apparatus of the United States is undergoing a phase of deep privatization. Institutions that previously provided a system of checks and balances now directly serve the interests of the ruling family and corporate donors. Attorney General nominee Todd Blanche formally rejected a $1.8 billion fund for January 6 protestors, yet guarantees President Trump and his business structures lifetime immunity from IRS audits, saving them hundreds of millions of dollars.

Simultaneously, the president's family launched a tech tool called "Truth API," offering millisecond insider access to the president's market-moving social media posts for a subscription fee of up to $100,000 per month. Top fundraiser Meredith O'Rourke uses methods akin to direct corporate racketeering, extracting over $800 million from corporations (including $50 million from SoftBank, $25 million from Apple, and $10 million from Meta) in exchange for favorable regulatory decisions, such as the elimination of tariffs on diamonds. Government decisions have become a commercial product available to hedge funds and mega-corporations.

04

THE TRUE COST OF REGLOBALIZATION FOR THE WESTERN CONSUMER

Cause → Effect: The deliberate destruction of the US-China trade axis and massive restructuring of global supply chains → The institutionalization of permanent inflation and the transfer of the costs of geopolitical confrontation to the end consumer in the West.

FedEx CEO Raj Subramaniam notes a fundamental shift: the company is recording a drop in US imports alongside a rise in exports, coupled with explosive growth in Latin America, India, and Southeast Asia. This process, dubbed "reglobalization," is framed in the press as a victory over dependence on China and successful diversification.

In reality, the duplication of supply chains (reshoring and friendshoring) destroys the deflationary effect the Western economy has enjoyed for the past 30 years. Moving production from China to regions with less developed infrastructure requires massive capital investments, which are inevitably baked into the final cost of goods. The middle class of the West are the losers, for whom the era of cheap consumer goods has ended forever.

SOCIALIZING COSTS UNDER THE GUISE OF ELDERCARE

Cause → Effect: Politicians promoting the concept of "free social care" for the elderly (the dementia tax) → A covert subsidization to preserve the inherited capital of the upper-middle class by increasing the tax burden on the working population.

UK politician Andy Burnham is pushing an initiative for a universal social care system for the elderly, free at the point of use, which would cost the budget £18-20 billion annually. Publicly, this is presented as a humanistic abolition of the "dementia tax"—the situation where elderly individuals are forced to sell their homes to pay for nursing home stays.

A colossal logical gap exists here. Under the current system, assets are only seized from those whose wealth exceeds £23,250, and the elderly receive care until the end of their lives regardless of their ability to pay. The true goal of the campaign is not to protect sick seniors, but to protect the financial expectations of their adult children in the middle and upper classes, who view inheriting their parents' unencumbered real estate as a "basic right."

"PEOPLE'S CAPITALISM" AS A DEFENSE AGAINST SOCIAL REVOLUTION

Cause → Effect: The launch of state programs to distribute micro-capital (investment accounts) to newborns or workers → An attempt by the financial oligarchy to prevent mass radicalization and legitimize its overconsumption by creating the illusion of the poor's involvement in the stock market.

Billionaire Bill Ackman is actively lobbying for the "birthright accounts" initiative, which resonated with the Trump administration in the form of "Trump Accounts" ($1,000 to every newborn) and "American Dream Accounts." The narrative claims that compound interest will make every American a millionaire by retirement, thereby realizing the American Dream.

The hidden structure of interests is transparent: elites realize that 40% of the US population owns no stock and harbors growing hatred for the capitalist system with every stock market rally. Handing out small sums, locked until retirement age, cannot mathematically offset the rising costs of living, healthcare, and real estate. However, it creates a powerful psychological barrier against radical left politicians demanding real redistribution of the tax burden and nationalization.

WHAT REMAINS UNSAID

ROBOTICS • LABOR • ECOLOGY
08

THE HIDDEN US DEFEAT IN PHYSICAL ROBOTICS

Cause → Effect: The FCC's ban on importing Chinese anthropomorphic robots under the pretext of a national security threat ("Trojan horses") → A systemic suppression of the critical technological and manufacturing lag of the US behind China in the field of embodied AI.

The media focus is directed at the threat to privacy: allegedly, Chinese robots will spy on Americans in their homes. Systemically suppressed is a fundamental economic fact: the United States has lost the race to scale physical robotics. American startups (Agility Robotics, Boston Dynamics, Figure AI) create cutting-edge software, but it is practically impossible to manufacture a competitive anthropomorphic robot without Chinese components and production facilities.

While China dominates mass production, orders for industrial robots within the US fell from a peak of 44,196 units in 2022 to 36,766 last year. The FCC ban is not an act of defense, but an act of protectionist desperation, hiding the reality that the next industrial revolution—the replacement of human labor by physical AI—will be controlled by PRC production lines.

09

LABOR ADAPTATION TO AI DOES NOT SOLVE INEQUALITY

Cause → Effect: The successful adaptation of corporations to AI integration through worker retraining (the IKEA case) → Obscuring the fact that automation marginalizes independent labor, rigidly binding workers to corporate means of production.

IKEA introduced the AI bot "Billie," which took over 74% of routine customer requests. Instead of layoffs, the company retrained 8,500 call center employees to become interior design consultants, leading to a 15-20% annual growth in remote sales (to $1.37 billion).

The weak signal hidden in this success story: AI doesn't kill jobs directly, but it transitions a human from being a bearer of unique knowledge to an operator of a corporate "human-machine" system. Workers not integrated into corporate AI infrastructures will inevitably be pushed out of the market. Successful retraining within IKEA masks the global threat of skill devaluation for those who do not control the algorithms.

EXPORTING TECH COSTS TO SPACE AND CRIMINALIZING ECO-PROTEST

Cause → Effect: Corporate plans (SpaceX, Blue Origin) to create "orbital data centers" amidst the tightening of repressive DOJ policies against grassroots activists → The export of toxic ecological costs of the AI revolution beyond Earth's jurisdiction while harshly suppressing any social resistance domestically.

Public attention is fixated on the construction of data centers on Earth. However, corporations are planning to place data centers in orbit to bypass terrestrial environmental regulations. Satellites re-entering the atmosphere are already polluting the stratosphere with aluminum oxide and black soot at alarming rates, ignoring the federal NEPA law.

Simultaneously with exporting pollution to space, the state is criminalizing domestic protest. Take the case of Eric Davis, a 53-year-old Buddhist professor from Minnesota facing 6 years in prison for "conspiracy" for coordinating peaceful protests against ICE raids. Prosecutors have no evidence of violence but use the "Antifa" label and private Signal messages to build a criminal case. A colossal imbalance is forming: massive ecological damage from orbital constellations goes unpunished, while civil activism is suppressed with preemptive prison sentences.

A MISSED DIPLOMATIC FORK IN THE MIDDLE EAST

The military crisis around the Strait of Hormuz was not a historical inevitability. It was the direct result of the Trump administration's political need to project toughness before elections, using Iran as a foreign policy irritant. The United States could have initiated diplomatic de-escalation before the massive exchange of missile strikes began. This would have preserved the strategic military reserve of precision weapons and air defense systems (Patriot and THAAD).

However, the doctrine of "maximum pressure," which excluded the use of ground troops to minimize casualties, forced the Pentagon to rely exclusively on remote precision strikes. The rigid manufacturing limits of the US defense industry clashed fatally with political ambitions. Intelligence failures regarding Iran's pain threshold narrowed the choices to nuclear escalation or a covert capitulation in Oman, resulting in the loss of control over the strait.

ATTEMPTED PRIVATIZATION OF GLOBAL SPORTS AND CORPORATE LIMITS

FORECAST: SCENARIOS FOR 2027-2030

Based on our analysis of structural shifts, we project the following outcomes for the global political and economic landscape through 2030:

  • Almost Inevitable: Legalization of corruption rent and the oligarchization of US power.
    Lobbying in its classical sense will cease to exist, giving way to the direct purchase of state services. Legislative and regulatory decisions will openly correlate with corporate contributions. The judicial system will legalize this practice, effectively providing legal immunity for the ruling group while prosecuting political opponents. Public trust in federal institutions will plummet to historic lows, sparking a rise in regional separatism regarding taxation and law enforcement.
  • Probable: Global "AI winter" for startups and the absolute monopoly of infrastructure giants.
    Venture capital will flee the consumer AI software segment en masse. Only corporations that physically control data centers, cooling systems, power generation, and silicon chip production will survive and multiply their strength. A global cartel of 3-4 transnational companies will form, becoming the exclusive provider of cognitive and computing power, dictating terms to national governments with influence akin to the oil monopolies of the early 20th century.
  • Unlikely but Dangerous: Massive paralysis of US critical infrastructure.
    If a synchronized sabotage (cyber + physical) occurs on US territory by proxy groups while domestic air defenses are depleted, unprecedented panic will ensue. This would lead to an immediate collapse of financial markets, martial law, and severe crackdowns on any "suspicious" domestic groups. The US would be forced into an emergency withdrawal of remaining troops from Europe and the Asia-Pacific to defend the homeland, creating an instant geopolitical vacuum and regional wars in Eurasia.
  • Wild Card: Synchronous climate-social collapse of statehood in the West.
    Should unprecedented climate disasters overlap with the economic degradation of the bottom 40% of the population and the total dysfunction of political elites, two-party consensuses will cascade into failure. Governments, having spent resources on bailing out stock markets and funding foreign wars, will find themselves completely lacking the financial or coercive resources to maintain territorial integrity. The formation of new, radical forms of statehood (eco-authoritarian or ultra-right corporate) will begin on the ruins of Western democracies.
