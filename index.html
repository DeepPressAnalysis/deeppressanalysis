THE MACRO-LEVEL LEGITIMIZATION

The institutional consensus is currently legitimizing the total militarization of human consciousness, the algorithmic management of citizen behavior, and the structural expropriation of private financial assets under the pretext of protecting democracy from hybrid threats, ensuring social welfare, and preventing the collapse of the global financial system.

SEMANTIC HIJACKING

LINGUISTIC ENGINEERING • DOUBLESPEAK
02

MILITARY & INFORMATION SPHERE

To implement regimes of resource and rights extraction at the macro level, authorities employ strict linguistic engineering, known in academic literature as "doublespeak." This mechanism is designed to conceal, distort, or invert the actual meaning of managerial actions to prevent the cognitive resistance of the masses.1 Semantic hijacking operates through the construction of political euphemisms that shift focus away from functional consequences toward abstract moral imperatives.4 In the current institutional configuration, we observe a synchronous hijacking of the conceptual apparatus across three critical spheres.

Old Terminology → New Terminology: Censorship, propaganda, and psychological operations (PsyOps) → Cognitive Domain Operations and Cognitive Security.

Emotional Anchor: Existential fear of external threats, hybrid attacks, disinformation, and the loss of state sovereignty. The fundamental need of society for safety and stability in an increasingly complex information landscape is actively exploited.6

Exclusion of Alternatives: NATO doctrinal documents explicitly postulate that in the 21st century, the human brain is both the target and the primary theater of war.6 The discourse is constructed in such a way that any information influence not sanctioned by the state is branded as an attack on the nation's cognitive infrastructure.8 Within this paradigm, an individual acting against the power structures' monopoly on content moderation is automatically stigmatized—not as a political opponent, but as a victim of disinformation or an agent of hostile influence undermining the "resilience" of democracy.7

The integration of the cognitive warfare concept into official rhetoric allows authorities to bypass traditional legal constraints. While Information Warfare previously focused on destroying physical communication infrastructure or mass broadcasting, cognitive warfare shifts the focus directly to fundamental decision-making processes, emotions, beliefs, and trust.8 This decriminalizes methods of social engineering when they are applied by the state against its own citizens in the name of "protecting vulnerabilities."8

03

SOCIAL & ADMINISTRATIVE SPHERE

Old Terminology → New Terminology: Social engineering, bureaucratic dictate, and manipulation → Behavioral Insights, Choice Architecture, and Nudge.

Emotional Anchor: Concern for the health of future generations, the financial well-being of citizens, environmental sustainability, and improving the efficiency of public service delivery.15

Exclusion of Alternatives: The implementation of behavioral economics at the state level is presented as an evidence-based policy. The discourse strictly excludes the very possibility of critique: the refusal of "correct" behavior, as constructed by choice architects, is declared to be the consequence of cognitive biases and the irrationality of the citizen themselves.17 Dissent is pathologized. Because "Nudging" formally does not forbid alternatives, but merely makes them psychologically uncomfortable or invisible, any discontent is viewed as marginal and anti-social.19

04

FINANCIAL & ECONOMIC SPHERE

In the financial sector, semantic hijacking is aimed at legitimizing the redistribution of corporate losses onto the shoulders of the public, which required unprecedented linguistic acrobatics from regulators and central banks.

Old Terminology → New Terminology: Confiscation of deposits, writing off obligations, and budget-funded rescues (Bail-out / Confiscation) → Internal settlement mechanism (Bail-in / Orderly Resolution).

Emotional Anchor: Exploitation of the average voter's class hatred toward Wall Street and financial elites. A sense of restored justice is manufactured: the narrative claims that from now on, the losses of collapsed banks will be paid for by the bankers themselves and their creditors, not the taxpayers.21

Exclusion of Alternatives: The discourse is strictly limited to a false dichotomy: either a catastrophic collapse of the entire global financial system leading to uncontrolled panic, or a "controlled resolution" via bail-in.21 The fact that ordinary bank clients fall into the category of "unsecured creditors" subject to a "haircut" and forced conversion into equity is heavily masked by complex legal terminology.23 Any criticism of the bail-in mechanism is equated to irresponsibly supporting the flawed practice of bail-outs, which historically shifted costs onto the state budget.

ARCHITECTURE OF CONSENT

THINK TANKS • MEDIA • BENEFICIARIES
05

THINK TANKS: THE IDEOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK

The production of institutional consent requires seamless integration between the production of ideologemes, their conceptualization, and their mass distribution. The revealed echo chamber relies on the Herman-Chomsky propaganda model (Manufacturing Consent), adapted for the digital age, where structural filters (media ownership, advertising budgets, sourcing, flak, and fear) ensure the dominance of desired narratives.26

The ideological framework for the extraction of subjectivity and resources is constructed within elite analytical centers (Think Tanks), which act as monopolists of expert knowledge:

  • RAND Corporation: Functions as a key intellectual incubator for the US military-industrial complex, conducting research through FFRDC centers (Federally Funded Research and Development Centers) such as the Arroyo Center and Project AIR FORCE.28 In a series of reports evaluating the military potential of China and the US, RAND introduces and conceptualizes the terms "cognitive domain" and "next-generation psychological warfare."30 By analyzing the doctrine of the People's Liberation Army of China (PLA) and their concept of the "Three Warfares,"33 RAND forms the justification for a mirror expansion of the mandate of US and NATO intelligence agencies to operate within the cognitive field of civil society, legitimizing the development of algorithmic and neurobiological weapons under the pretext of parity.29 The inevitability of cognitive warfare is solidified in NATO ACT reports, which emphasize that new technologies allow for the synchronization of "emotionally charged narratives with geopolitical events."7
  • Behavioural Insights Team (BIT): Created in 2010 within the UK Cabinet Office, this group, known as the Nudge Unit, released the fundamental report "MINDSPACE: Influencing behaviour through public policy."35 This document became a manual for bureaucracy to use nine heuristic triggers of the human psyche to manage behavior.16 BIT's success in generating "cheap consent" led to the global expansion of the concept: similar Nudge laboratories were integrated into the governments of dozens of countries and international organizations (OECD, World Bank).35
  • Chatham House and Brookings Institution: These centers, possessing profound influence over foreign and geoeconomic policy, form elite consensus behind closed doors (institutionalizing the "Chatham House Rule").28 Receiving funding from energy corporations, the military-industrial complex, and foreign governments, they produce white papers that legitimize new macroeconomic models—including the necessity for aggressive bank resolution mechanisms without the use of public funds, conceptually paving the way for the BRRD directive.28
06

DELIVERY CHANNELS & MEDIA SYNCHRONIZATION

The transmission of concepts from the offices of analytical centers to mass consciousness is executed through the "revolving door" mechanism.28 Former government officials become researchers at Brookings or CSIS, and RAND experts assume posts in the Pentagon.44 This symbiosis allows for the simultaneous injection of talking points through affiliated media conglomerates, where think tank analysts act in the role of "objective experts" (Chomsky's sourcing filter).26

In the digital age, delivery channels are reinforced by algorithmic demotion.6 Fact-checking institutions (for example, within the IFCN framework), closely tied to these very analytical centers, execute hidden content moderation: narratives resisting Nudge policies or criticizing bail-in mechanisms are not physically deleted, but are algorithmically stripped of their reach (flak).6 Technical duplication (synchronized media) guarantees that the exact same linguistic constructs ("the necessity of burden-sharing", "protecting cognitive sovereignty") appear in thousands of sources simultaneously.50

BENEFICIARIES OF THE CONSENSUS

The echo chamber serves the interests of a conglomerate striving to consolidate power while minimizing political responsibility:

  • The Military-Industrial Complex and National Security Apparatus: Legitimizing "cognitive warfare" allows for the expansion of budgets for research in artificial intelligence, neurobiology, and social media monitoring.6 The civilian population is officially recognized as an attack vector, which justifies the merging of intelligence agencies with Big Tech to control information flows.6
  • The Global Financial Sector and Central Banks: The legalization of the bail-in mechanism via the BRRD Directive in Europe and the Dodd-Frank Act in the US frees elites from having to answer for systemic risks, shifting costs onto unsecured creditors (clients) and protecting derivative markets.21
  • State Bureaucracy: Nudge technologies allow the state to achieve target metrics (e.g., tax collection, consumption reduction, organ donor registration) with zero political cost, completely avoiding open debate and legislative coercion.19

THE HIDDEN TARGET

COGNITIVE • BEHAVIORAL • FINANCIAL
08

COGNITIVE EXPROPRIATION

To understand the anatomy of cognitive hegemony, one must strictly differentiate between the facade of PR promises handed down to the masses and the actual power functions implemented through the created instruments. The analysis reveals three parallel tracks of extraction: cognitive, behavioral, and financial.

The Facade: At the public level, society consumes the product of ideal paternalism. In the military sphere, the construct of "cognitive security" promises the protection of citizens from foreign disinformation, troll farms, and psychological wear-down, preserving democratic institutions intact.6

The Reality (The OODA Loop Hijack): The functional goal of the operation is the dismantling of civic subjectivity. The true objective of the cognitive warfare concept, developed within NATO and RAND, is internal control.6 By defining the cognitive sphere as the sixth domain of warfare, military institutions transfer Psychological Operations (PsyOps) methods from foreign adversaries directly onto their own populations.6

The mechanics are built on hijacking John Boyd's OODA Loop (Observe-Orient-Decide-Act).52 The state seeks to generate "acute" effects (short-term disruptions in perception) and "chronic" effects (long-term loss of trust in alternative institutions).54 Utilizing neurotechnologies, AI, and attentional saturation, the system creates "fog, fear, and friction," stripping the subject of their ability to adequately orient themselves.8 In this reality, oppositional discourse is not simply debated; it is deconstructed at the level of neural connections, and censorship becomes an integral function of maintaining combat readiness.12

09

BEHAVIORAL DICTATE & THE NUDGE ILLUSION

The Facade: In the sphere of public policy, the MINDSPACE framework and the concept of Nudge are positioned as a caring, invisible hand that helps flawed human beings (prone to error) make beneficial decisions in complex situations, without restricting their freedom of choice.20

The Reality: The implementation of the MINDSPACE framework signifies the complete abandonment of rational dialogue with society by the elites.16 Driving factors, such as Defaults (leveraging inertia), Messenger (manipulating source authority), Affect (emotional triggers), and Norms (herd mentality), are targeted exclusively at Daniel Kahneman's "System 1"—the fast, automatic, non-reflective brain.16

However, the true cynicism of behavioral economics is revealed in its statistical failure. Recent global meta-analyses, adjusted for publication bias, prove that the actual effect of Nudge instruments on a state scale is microscopic—averaging 1.4%, which is six times lower than initially claimed academic metrics (8.7%).20 If Nudge doesn't work as advertised, why do governments invest in it?

The true purpose (Power) is to create political cover.20 By shifting focus to the micro-behavior of the individual (i-frame), elites evade executing complex, costly structural reforms (s-frame)—such as legislative changes, restricting corporate monopolies, or raising taxes on capital.20 The individualization of guilt allows the system to write off systemic failures as the irrationality of the citizens themselves.

10

FINANCIAL CONFISCATION

The Facade: Laws concerning bank restructuring promise the ultimate deliverance of taxpayers from the blackmail of "Too Big To Fail" institutions, transferring responsibility onto the shoulders of the capitalists themselves.21

The Reality (The Bail-in Confiscation): The BRRD Directive in the EU and the Dodd-Frank Act (Title II - Orderly Liquidation Authority) in the US, implemented post-2008 crisis, legalize outright theft under the guise of financial rehabilitation.21 The bail-in mechanism grants regulators statutory power during weekends, without a court order, to write off the unsecured obligations of a failing bank or convert them into devalued capital.23

The stark reality is that ordinary corporate accounts of businesses and private deposits of individuals exceeding the insured minimum are legally classified as those very unsecured credits.23 Simultaneously, derivative instruments generated on Wall Street receive immunity from bail-ins and retain the right to demand immediate collateral backing.23 Thus, the systemic risks created by speculative capital will be extinguished using the real money of depositors. The 2013 Cyprus precedent and the 2017 liquidation of Banco Popular vividly demonstrated the anatomy of this extraction.21
