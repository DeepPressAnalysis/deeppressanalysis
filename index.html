01

THE OVERTON SHIFT: SEMANTIC HIJACKING

The institutional consensus right now is legitimizing the total militarization of civilian budgets, the privatization of cyberspace by corporations, and the implementation of algorithmic censorship. This is done under the pretext of defending against cognitive attacks, containing agentic artificial intelligence, and overcoming global geoeconomic fragmentation.

A structural analysis of official documents, strategic reviews, and analytical memos for the period 2025–2026 demonstrates a massive and coordinated operation in semantic recoding. The language of state and transnational governance has been deliberately altered to mask the processes of extracting public resources, dismantling social guarantees, and establishing preemptive control over digital discourse. Power no longer operates with terms like "restriction of freedoms" or "spending cuts"; it constructs a new vocabulary in which militarization becomes synonymous with survival, and censorship becomes synonymous with hygiene.

To ensure analytical clarity, the primary vectors of this semantic hijacking are structured as follows:

  • Censorship and suppression of dissent is now framed as Cognitive Security and Anti-FIMI. This masks the transfer of information control from the political sphere to national security, where martial law applies.
  • Cutting social and civil budgets is now framed as European Competitiveness. This masks the transfer of hundreds of billions of euros from development and science funds into subsidies for defense conglomerates (EDIP, ECF).
  • Offensive AI arms race is now framed as Agentic Cyber Strategy and Tokenpolitik. This justifies the aggressive deployment of autonomous algorithmic systems (Red Kraken) as the sole means of deterring a geopolitical adversary.
  • Local wars and imperialism is now framed as Escalation management and Defending democratic resilience. This legitimizes proxy conflicts and expanding military powers under the guise of containing "epistemological chaos" and hybrid threats.
02

EMOTIONAL ANCHORING: THE FEAR PARADIGM

The emotional anchor of this consensus is the fear of systemic paralysis of critical infrastructure, cascading social collapse, and the loss of basic security. Think tanks and narrative incubators masterfully construct multi-layered disaster scenarios.

The World Economic Forum (WEF) in its Global Risks Report 2026 solidifies "misinformation and disinformation" and "geoeconomic confrontation" as dominant short-term threats, while simultaneously anchoring public consciousness to the long-term fear of climate catastrophe and extreme weather events. This permanent "age of competition" is presented as a time of structural instability, where historical data can no longer serve as a guide for predicting the future.

Parallelly, the Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) scales this fear through wargames, such as the "Red Kraken" simulation. Society and politicians are shown a scenario in which a hostile AI agent collapses West Coast logistics, paralyzing ports, rail hubs, and aviation by corrupting the data upon which decision-making is based. The emotion of absolute vulnerability to an invisible, autonomous, and intelligent algorithmic enemy paralyzes rational societal resistance to the transfer of powers and budgets. Fear becomes the primary driver of consent.

03

COGNITIVE WARFARE AND THE EXCLUSION OF ALTERNATIVES

The discourse is structured in such a way that any political or social opposition to growing defense budgets, tightening platform control, or transferring sovereignty to supranational structures is automatically marginalized and criminalized.

In the paradigm being developed by NATO structures and RAND analysts, the concept of "Cognitive Warfare" is introduced. According to this doctrine, the human brain, emotions, and social cohesion are officially recognized as a "theater of war." Cognitive warfare is divided into biological (impact on the nervous system), psychological (manipulation of framing), and social (destruction of institutional legitimacy) levels.

The consequence of this doctrine is catastrophic for any dissent: if society is the attack surface, then any citizen questioning the expediency of purchasing "Golden Dome" missile defense systems for $3.6 trillion, or opposing the reduction of EU civilian funds for the AGILE program, is declared a victim of cognitive distortion or a "useful idiot" relaying Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference (FIMI). Anyone who opposes the consensus is no longer considered a political opponent—they are classified as a national security breach requiring "cognitive hygiene" and "deplatforming."

04

ARCHITECTURE OF CONSENT: THINK TANKS

The synchronization of narratives observed in the 2025–2026 document array is not the result of an organic evolution of public opinion. It is the consequence of a highly coordinated closed echo chamber consisting of global think tanks, transnational media, state bureaucracies, and corporate institutions. This architecture operates on a principle of cascading distribution: from closed analytical concepts (white papers) to public media campaigns, and finally to mandatory legislation. The production of primary meanings is strictly divided among key institutions, each responsible for its sector of forming the "inevitable norm."

  • World Economic Forum (WEF): Responsible for the macro-framework and global goal-setting consensus. Through the Global Risks Report 2026, the Forum legitimizes the world's transition into a state of fragmentation and polycrisis. The report, based on a survey of 1,300 "experts," directively designates disinformation and geoeconomic shocks as the primary threats requiring a "rethinking of media literacy" and the creation of new "coalitions of the willing."
  • Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS): Acts as the operator of tactical military planning and budget legitimation for the United States. CSIS produces coordinated insertions of concepts, such as "Tokenpolitik" (using digital tokens as the currency of the new international AI order) and "Red Kraken" (simulating AI attacks on critical logistics). It is CSIS that analyzes and legitimizes the new 2026 National Defense Strategy (2026 NDS), justifying the renaming of the Department of Defense to the Department of War, the shift in focus to hemisphere defense, the creation of the exorbitant "Golden Dome," and the expansion of the defense budget to $1.5 trillion.
  • RAND Corporation and Chatham House: Serve as strategic rationalizers for the European and British theaters. RAND reports from 2025–2026 (e.g., "Spotlight 2025–2026" and documents for the UK Strategic Defence Review) justify the necessity of merging defense and civilian industrial sectors, expanding the capabilities of generative AI in Pentagon psychological operations, and preparing the population for hybrid shocks. Chatham House solidifies the narrative regarding the decline of the Western liberal model and the need for harsh geoeconomic containment of the Global South.
05

CHANNELS OF DELIVERY & LEGISLATIVE INTEGRATION

To transform theories into inevitable reality, a synchronized infrastructure of legalization is employed. At the global level, WEF theses are broadcast through the Davos Forum in January 2026, where roughly 3,000 elite representatives from 130 countries gather. This macro-narrative is instantly replicated by corporate media, legalizing the fear of a "halt in global growth due to wars."

At the level of tactical implementation in the US, CSIS utilizes direct wargaming mechanisms with members of Congress (e.g., with the House Committee on Homeland Security and the Select Committee on China). By terrifying lawmakers with the "Red Kraken" simulation, the think tank ensures the unhindered passage of acts like the PILLAR Act and the Guaranteeing Universal Access to Cybersecurity Act.

In the European Union, narrative delivery is executed through the bureaucratic regulations of the European Commission. Doctrinal justifications are converted into binding directives: the European Defence Industrial Strategy (EDIS), the European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP), and initiatives like AGILE. These documents, which do not require broad public approval, are integrated into the Multiannual Financial Framework (MFF), creating an established fact of budget reallocation. The Canadian Security Intelligence Service (CSIS) simultaneously supports this trend through its 2025-2026 public reports, declaring national security a "shared responsibility" and expanding its mandate to combat transnational repression.

The structural integration of European legitimation initiatives can be broken down functionally:

  • European Competitiveness Fund (ECF): The declared goal is technological leadership. The functional result is the merging of civilian and military budgets totaling €409 billion.
  • Security Action for Europe (SAFE): The declared goal is supporting national capabilities. The functional result is the issuance of secured loans worth €150 billion, vastly increasing the debt burden.
  • AGILE Innovation Programme: The declared goal is rapid cycles of defense innovation. The functional result is the reallocation of Small and Medium-sized Enterprise (SME) support funds in favor of dual-use startups.

06

THE HIDDEN TARGET: FACADE VS. REALITY

The separation of declared (PR) and functional (Power) goals exposes a mechanism of unprecedented resource expropriation and the quiet dismantling of political freedoms. Institutional propaganda masks this extraction under the guise of salvation.

The Facade: The public is indoctrinated to believe that the state and supranational structures (EU, NATO) are mobilizing solely to protect the lives and well-being of citizens. The average person is promised security from cyber catastrophes, the prevention of supply chain collapses, and the defense of democracy from the corrupting influence of disinformation and autocratic manipulation (FIMI). Under the aegis of EDIS and ECF, Europeans are promised a "revival of industrial competitiveness," autonomy from external technological shocks, and the integration of deep tech and clean energy. Bills like the PILLAR Act in the US are sold as assistance to local budgets in protecting schools, hospitals, and fire stations from ransomware hackers.

The Reality: Behind the facade of "cognitive security" and "defense integration" hides a massive, structural expropriation of financial and political capital.

  • Budgetary Expropriation and Debt Slavery: Under the cover of EDIS strategies and the ECF initiative, a silent seizure of civilian funds is occurring in the European Union. Money from the Horizon Europe program and cohesion funds, originally intended to equalize living standards across regions, is being redirected to direct subsidies for arms conglomerates and dual-use startups. The creation of the €150 billion SAFE credit instrument means that the costs of militarization will be paid by future generations of taxpayers through severe austerity and inflation, while the defense industry already acknowledges a catastrophic workforce shortage and prohibitive energy prices.
  • Structural Privatization of Cyberspace: Acts like PILLAR and programs like the Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center (MS-ISAC) in the US effectively coerce local governments into surrendering control of their digital infrastructure to commercial contractors. Municipalities are made dependent on constant subscriptions to corporate Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) services and cloud AI, paid for by federal grants. Once the grants run out, local budgets will find themselves hostage to private monopolies.
  • Criminalization of Cognitive Processes: The conceptualization of "Cognitive Warfare" shifts the battle against dissent from a legal plane to a neurobiological and psychological one. The need for "interpretation management" and the "protection of cohesion," as declared by NATO and research centers, legitimizes the creation of systems for the total algorithmic monitoring of sentiment. The real goal is not so much protection from foreign bots as it is the preemptive suppression of any internal political self-organization capable of threatening the new economic consensus of the military-industrial complex and Big Tech. The discourse on "sovereignty" and "protection against disinformation" is used to justify censorship at the architectural level of digital platforms themselves.
07

ACTIONABLE VERDICT & THE CLIENTS OF CONSENSUS

The architecture of consent operates in the interests of a symbiotic alliance between the transnational military-industrial complex (MIC) and the technology sector (Big Tech). The MIC in the US (receiving guarantees for munitions procurement and the construction of the Golden Dome system) and Europe (forming the basis for EDIS and gaining access to cohesion funds) requires long-term, institutionally protected contracts independent of electoral cycles. Big Tech and cybersecurity conglomerates are the beneficiaries of the "agentic cyber warfare" concept. Mandatory algorithmic protection of infrastructure, pushed through acts like PILLAR, guarantees private corporations a monopoly on providing cloud solutions, authentication systems, and Threat Intelligence for municipal and state structures. The financial sector (including InvestEU) accumulates commissions from the reallocation of the €150 billion SAFE credit lines.

Actionable Verdict: The legitimation operation is in its active phase. Society has been prepared for an unprecedented militarization of state governance and the structural integration of algorithmic censorship systems. The costs of this operation—economic stagnation, inflation, the dismantling of social funds, and the forced expropriation of civilian budgets amounting to hundreds of billions of dollars and euros—will be shifted onto the middle class, municipalities, and taxpayers. Meanwhile, political dividends and capital will be accumulated by a transnational cartel of the military-industrial complex, tech giants, and supranational bureaucracy, operating entirely behind the facade of defending against cognitive, climate, and geoeconomic threats.
